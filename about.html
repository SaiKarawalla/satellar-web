About SATellar

We built SATellar because SAT prep shouldn't cost thousands of dollars or feel like a chore. Most students don't need a private tutor or a $2,000 course — they need consistent practice with questions that actually match their level, and a clear picture of where they stand.

SATellar uses adaptive technology to figure out what you're good at and what needs work, then serves you the right questions at the right difficulty. Your predicted score updates after every session, so you can see real progress — not just "you completed 50 questions."

We're a small team of engineers and educators who think test prep can be better. No gimmicks, no filler content, no guilt-tripping you into upgrading. Just a clean app that helps you practice, track your growth, and walk into test day feeling ready.
