Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information

SATellar does not sell or share your personal information with third parties for advertising, marketing, or any other commercial purpose.

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA), California residents have the right to opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of their personal information.

We want to be clear about our practices:

We do not sell your personal information. We have never sold personal information and have no plans to do so.

We do not share your personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising. We do not work with advertising networks or data brokers.

We do not use advertising trackers. Our app and website do not contain third-party advertising SDKs, tracking pixels, or behavioral profiling tools.

The only third parties that receive your data are our essential service providers (Supabase for authentication and database hosting, Apple and Google for payment processing), and they are contractually prohibited from using your data for their own purposes. See our Privacy Policy for full details.

Your Rights

Even though we don't sell or share your data, you still have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal information we hold about you
  • Request deletion of your personal information
  • Request correction of inaccurate information

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at:

Email: support@satellar.app
Response time: Within 45 days of receiving your verified request
No account required: You do not need to create an account to submit a request

We will not discriminate against you for exercising any of your privacy rights.
