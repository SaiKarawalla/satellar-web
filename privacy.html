Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 22, 2026 • Effective: April 22, 2026

The short version: SATellar runs entirely on your device. We don't create accounts for you, we don't collect your name or email, we don't sync your practice data to any server, and we don't sell or share anything. Your practice history, streaks, and stats live only on the phone you installed the app on. Uninstalling the app removes them. Purchases go through Apple's in-app billing, validated by RevenueCat; we never see your payment card details. If you're under 13, you may not use SATellar.

1. Who We Are

SATellar is an independent mobile application for practicing the Digital SAT. This Privacy Policy explains how the SATellar iOS application ("App") and our website at https://satellar.app (collectively, the "Service") handle your information.

SAT® is a registered trademark of College Board, which is not affiliated with, and does not endorse, SATellar.

For privacy questions, contact us at: support@satellar.app

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide Directly to the App

When you first open SATellar, you enter a display name. That's the only thing you provide. We do not ask for, and do not collect, your email address, password, phone number, date of birth, or any other identifying information.

Data TypeWhen CollectedWhere It Lives
Display nameWhen you first open the AppOnly on your device (local storage)

2.2 Information Generated Through Your Use of the App

As you practice, the App generates activity data. This data is stored only on your device (using local SQLite storage) and is never transmitted to us.

Data TypeDescription
Practice session dataQuestions answered, answer selections, scores, time spent, session type
Performance dataAccuracy rates, difficulty level, subject-area performance, predicted score
BookmarksQuestions you save for review
Badges & achievementsEarned based on your activity
PreferencesTheme (light/dark), notification settings, streak data

2.3 Information Processed by Third Parties When You Make a Purchase

If you purchase a subscription or a one-time session pack, the following limited information is processed by Apple and by RevenueCat (our subscription-management provider). See Section 4.2 for detail.

  • Apple receives: Your Apple ID and payment details (processed entirely by Apple; we never see your payment card details).
  • RevenueCat receives: An anonymous App-user identifier, your purchase receipt from Apple, your device platform/OS version, and your current subscription status. RevenueCat does not receive your name, email, practice data, or payment card details.

2.4 Information We Do NOT Collect

The App does not collect or transmit: email address, password, phone number, date of birth, precise location, approximate location, IP address (the App never contacts our servers), contacts, photos, microphone data, camera data, health data, crash reports with identifying information, or advertising identifiers.

3. How We Use Your Information

Because practice data never leaves your device, we do not use it for anything — it is processed only by the App on your phone, on your behalf.

Purchase data (processed by Apple and RevenueCat, not us directly) is used only to:

  • Complete your subscription purchase and grant access to Pro features.
  • Validate your purchase receipt and restore purchases on reinstall or on a new device signed in with the same Apple ID.
  • Detect fraud and duplicate purchases.

We will never sell your personal information. We do not use your data for third-party advertising. We do not build advertising profiles based on your activity. We do not track you across apps or websites.

4. How Your Information Is Shared

4.1 With Other Users (Leaderboard)

SATellar includes a leaderboard feature. Because practice data does not leave your device, no leaderboard data is shared between devices at this time. Your leaderboard standing is computed only from your own local activity. When friend-based leaderboards are introduced in a future version, this policy will be updated before that feature ships and only information you explicitly share (display name and overall score) would be transmitted, with your consent.

4.2 With Service Providers

ProviderPurposeData Shared
AppleApp Store payment processingApple ID, payment details (processed entirely by Apple; we do not see your payment card details)
RevenueCatSubscription receipt validation, entitlement tracking, restore purchasesAnonymous App-user identifier, Apple purchase receipt, device platform/OS version, subscription status

These providers process data on our behalf under contractual obligations of confidentiality. They do not receive your name, email, practice data, or any personally identifying information about you. See Apple's Privacy Policy and RevenueCat's Privacy Policy for how each of them handles data.

4.3 For Legal Reasons

Because we do not collect or store your personal data on any server, there is typically nothing for us to disclose in response to legal requests. If a legal request is directed at Apple or RevenueCat regarding your purchase, those companies will respond according to their own policies.

4.4 Business Transfers

If SATellar is involved in a merger, acquisition, or asset sale, information related to purchases (which lives with Apple and RevenueCat, not with us) may be transferred to the acquiring entity. Practice data remains on your device regardless.

5. Data Storage and Security

5.1 Where Your Data Is Stored

  • On your device: All practice data, preferences, display name, bookmarks, badges, and questions are stored locally using iOS's built-in SQLite database and local file storage (AsyncStorage).
  • With Apple: Your purchase transactions are handled by Apple per Apple's own privacy policy.
  • With RevenueCat: Purchase receipts and subscription status are stored on RevenueCat's servers (hosted in the United States) per RevenueCat's own privacy policy.

We do not operate our own servers and we do not have a database that contains your practice activity or display name.

5.2 International Data Transfers

Purchase-related data handled by RevenueCat is stored on servers in the United States. For users in the EEA or UK, RevenueCat relies on Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) as the legal mechanism for such transfers; see RevenueCat's privacy policy for detail.

5.3 Security Measures

  • All network communication between the App and Apple/RevenueCat is encrypted in transit (TLS/SSL).
  • Because there is no account system, there are no credentials to steal from us.
  • On-device data is protected by your device's own security (passcode, Face ID, Touch ID, disk encryption).

No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security of data handled by Apple or RevenueCat on their own systems; please refer to their policies.

7. Your Privacy Rights

7.1 All Users — How to Exercise Your Rights

Because your practice data never leaves your device, you have complete control over it at all times:

  • Access: All your data is visible in the App (profile, history, bookmarks).
  • Correct: Change your display name at any time in profile settings.
  • Delete: Uninstall the App (this removes all local data permanently) or use the App's reset-data option if available.
  • Subscription data: For data held by Apple or RevenueCat, contact them directly or email support@satellar.app and we will help route your request.

7.2 California Residents (CCPA/CPRA)

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act, as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act, California residents have the right to:

  • Right to Know: Request the categories and specific pieces of personal information we have collected, the sources, business purposes, and third parties with whom we share it. (Short answer: we collect a display name you enter; Apple and RevenueCat process your purchase data; no other personal information is collected.)
  • Right to Delete: Request deletion of your personal information. Local data is deleted by uninstalling the App. For purchase-related data held by Apple/RevenueCat, we will help route the request.
  • Right to Correct: Request correction of inaccurate personal information (display name is user-editable in the App).
  • Right to Opt Out of Sale/Sharing: We do not sell or share your personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising. This is a permanent practice.
  • Right to Limit Use of Sensitive Information: We do not use sensitive personal information.
  • Right to Non-Discrimination: We will not discriminate against you for exercising your rights.

How to exercise your rights: Email support@satellar.app. We will respond within 45 days.

In accordance with the California Online Privacy Protection Act: this policy is conspicuously linked from our App and website; we describe all categories of personal information collected and third parties with whom it is shared; and we honor "Do Not Track" browser signals by not engaging in cross-site tracking.

7.5 Other U.S. States

Residents of Virginia, Colorado, Connecticut, Utah, Texas, Oregon, Montana, and other states with privacy laws may have similar rights to those described in Section 7.2. To exercise them, contact support@satellar.app.

8. Children's Privacy (COPPA)

SATellar is designed for students aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

8.1 Age Minimum

SATellar does not require an account, and therefore we cannot enforce an age gate at registration. By using the App, you confirm that you are at least 13 years old. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child under 13 is using SATellar, uninstall the App from the child's device to remove all local data immediately.

8.2 Users Aged 13–15

  • Data minimization is built in by design — the only information you enter is a display name.
  • We do not send marketing or promotional communications to any user, including those under 16, because we do not collect email addresses.
  • Under CCPA/CPRA, we do not sell or share personal information of consumers under 16 (or anyone else).

8.3 Parental Rights

If you are a parent or guardian and you have concerns about your child's use of SATellar, contact us at support@satellar.app. Because all practice data lives on the device, uninstalling the App removes all data associated with use.

9. Cookies and Tracking

9.1 Mobile App

The SATellar mobile app does not use cookies. The App uses local device storage (SQLite and AsyncStorage) for app data and preferences.

9.2 Website

Our website at https://satellar.app is a static marketing site. It does not set cookies, does not use advertising cookies, does not use third-party tracking pixels, and does not engage in cross-site tracking. We honor "Do Not Track" browser signals by design, because we do not track.

10. In-App Purchases and Subscriptions

All payments are processed through the Apple App Store (iOS). We do not directly collect, process, or store your credit card or payment details. We receive only a transaction confirmation, purchase type, and subscription status via RevenueCat.

We use RevenueCat as a service provider to validate your purchase receipts with Apple, track your subscription tier, and support the "Restore Purchases" feature across reinstalls and new devices. RevenueCat receives only an anonymous user identifier, your platform purchase receipts, device platform/OS version, and subscription status. RevenueCat does not receive your name, email, or practice data. See Section 4.2 for details.

You manage and cancel subscriptions through your Apple ID settings (Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions → SATellar), or through the "Manage Subscription" link in the App. See our Terms of Service Section 5 for auto-renewal, cancellation, and refund information.

11. Data Breach Notification

Because we do not operate servers that store your personal data, a data breach at SATellar affecting your personal information is extremely unlikely. In the event of any incident affecting information handled by RevenueCat or the App itself, we will:

  • Notify affected users without unreasonable delay (and within 72 hours where required by GDPR)
  • Notify the California Attorney General if the incident affects 500+ California residents
  • Describe what happened, what data was affected, and what steps we are taking

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. When we make changes:

  • We will update the "Last Updated" date at the top.
  • For material changes (such as any future addition of optional server-side features like a friends leaderboard), we will notify you via in-app notification before the change takes effect and before any new data processing begins.
  • Your continued use of the Service after the effective date constitutes acceptance.
